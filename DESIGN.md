# DigiDreams Mini-POS Design Direction

## Visual Identity
- **Palette**: Earth tones - warm paper background (#f4ecdf), deep ink text (#241b15), accent terracotta (#b44a25)
- **Typography**: Geist Sans for interface, Fraunces for display - balanced professional readability
- **Mood**: Warm, artisanal, cafe-coworking hybrid - not sterile tech-default

## Three Dials
- **ENERGY 1**: Calm, professional - say hello clearly but not aggressively
- **RHYTHM 2**: Consistent with few breaks - grid-based but with intentional variation between sections
- **MOTION 1**: Hover states only - subtle, purposeful motion

## Core Principles
- Content-driven: every section serves the products and ordering flow
- No AI defaults: no gradients, glassmorphism, or decorative trends without purpose
- Accessible: keyboard-navigable, high contrast, screen-reader friendly
- Responsive: perfect mobile layout, no horizontal overflow

## Prohibited Patterns (Antislop)
- No em dashes in UI text (R-02)
- No generic blue-purple gradients (R-01)
- No pill-shaped everything (R-11)
- No "Get Started"/"Learn More" generic CTAs (R-15)
- No AI marketing buzzwords (R-16)
- No cloned popular product styles (R-30)
- No fabricated claims or statistics (R-17, R-36)
